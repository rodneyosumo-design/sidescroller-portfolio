Resilience & Story

The Philosophy of
Slum Runner

Growing up under scarce, high-barrier conditions teaches a developer the most crucial engineering skill of all: resourcefulness.

'Slum Runner' represents the journey of a creative coder. The exaggerated big head represents intellectual curiosity and dreams. The broken-down cars are structural barriers that don't work, and the police barricades represent institutional gatekeeping. Dodging these obstacles is not just gameplay—it is a metaphor for inventing custom solutions, utilizing building blocks, and leaping forward against all odds.
